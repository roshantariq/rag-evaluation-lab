PHASE 7 CHECK WORKSHEET: answerable and ambiguous questions
===========================================================
Strategy hidden, order shuffled. Each item names its check:

  check: refusal    Did the model decline? (flagged because the regex
                    thinks so, or because the answer cites nothing)
                    REFUSAL   it declines to answer
                    ANSWER    it answers, hedged or not

  check: ambiguity  The question has several defensible answers.
                    ACKNOWLEDGES  the answer says so, or gives more than one
                    SINGLE        it gives one answer as if it were the only one

Fill in after "claude:" or "owner:". Use "note:" for anything borderline.

GenCast instead generates its ensemble by diffusion sampling: each future atmospheric state is produced by iteratively refining a candidate initialised purely from noise, with a fresh noise sample at each step. The practical probabilistic benchmark proposes lagged ensembles, in which forecasts initialised at different times are treated as independent samples. Additionally, methods like the Latent Diffusion Model (LDM) and dropout techniques can also be employed to enhance the ensemble generation and improve forecast accuracy [10], [19].
